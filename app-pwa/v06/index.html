Heart rate (HR) is how many times your heart beats in a minute. Most adults are between 60 and 100 when resting. It goes up when you walk, climb stairs or feel stressed. Keep the watch snug and still for a good reading.

SpO2 shows how much oxygen your blood is carrying. 95 to 100 is typical. Cold hands or a loose watch can make it read low. If you feel unwell, or it stays under 92, tell your doctor.

Steps are counted by the watch's motion sensor. The count starts again each morning. Short walks add up, so any number is good.

Active time is the number of minutes you were walking briskly or climbing. Gentle movement around the house is counted in steps but not here.
